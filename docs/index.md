features:
  - icon: 🧠
    title: Understand, not just visualize
    details: Betweenness, PageRank, community detection, and connected-component analysis surface bridge notes, prominent notes, and topic communities - not just a nicer drawing of your links.
  - icon: 🧬
    title: Notes related by meaning, not just links
    details: Semantic clustering reads every note's own content with a local embedding model and groups notes that are about the same thing, even if nothing links them to each other.
  - icon: 🩺
    title: Structural diagnostics
    details: One panel flags orphaned notes, broken links, isolated clusters, and communities scattered across mismatched folders - each one click away from "show me on the graph," with a soft glow marking exactly which notes belong together.
  - icon: 🔍
    title: Named, reusable filters
    details: Build filters from tags, properties, folder, filename, text, link count, edit recency, activity, structure, bridging, prominence, connectivity, community, or semantic cluster - invert any of them, combine several with either/or logic.
  - icon: 🎨
    title: Color & size by your own rules
    details: The same criteria as filtering, applied as named, colored groups instead - so "everything tagged #project" or "this note's own community" gets its own color and size at a glance.
  - icon: 🧭
    title: Four layouts, find a path, or focus on one note
    details: Force-directed, Hierarchical, Radial, or Circular for the whole vault - trace the actual route between two notes, or focus in on just one note and its neighborhood, hops away.
  - icon: 🔖
    title: Save a setup, come back to it
    details: Save your current filters, coloring, layout, and focus as a named view - one click restores the exact same combination later.
  - icon: 🕰️
    title: Watch your vault grow
    details: The Timeline panel replays notes and links in the order they were created, at a duration and pace you pick.
  - icon: 🎛️
    title: Live appearance tuning
    details: Node/edge size, color, physics, edge shape, and label density are all adjustable while watching the graph react immediately.
  - icon: 🖱️
    title: Click, hover, drag
    details: Click a node to open that note, hover to highlight its connections, drag a note to pin it exactly where you want it.
